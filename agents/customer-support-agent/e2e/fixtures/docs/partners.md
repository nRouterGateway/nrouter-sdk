# Northwind Notes Partner Program

The Northwind Notes Partner Program provides technical and commercial benefits to registered partners, certified solution providers, and system integrators.

## Partner Referral Commission

Partners receive recurring referral compensation for clients onboarded to Northwind Notes.

Partner referral rate is 7731 basis points on all net subscription and usage revenue generated during the client organization's first twelve months of active subscription.

## Partner Support and Enablement

Partners receive dedicated partner engineering support, early access to new API features, and joint marketing opportunities. Contact your assigned partner manager or email partners@northwind.example for assistance.
